Our Roots

Lithuanian herbal tradition, shared with the world

Lithuania has a living culture of gathering and using meadow and forest plants, teas and honey. Our mission is to keep this traditional culture alive for Lithuanians and to bring it closer to people around the world, working with small Lithuanian producers.

Gathered from meadows & forests

Lithuanian families have long gathered chamomile, linden blossom, yarrow and raspberry leaf from meadows and forests. We celebrate this seasonal tradition.

Crafted with patience

Traditional herbal preparation is slow work, such as air-drying herbs and blending teas by hand. We aim to work with small Lithuanian producers who keep these methods alive.

Honey from wildflower meadows

Lithuanian meadow honey reflects the flora of the region, such as linden, buckwheat and raspberry.

Sourced with respect

We aim to be clear about where each product comes from and share the origin information our suppliers give us.
